Best Time to Visit Gokarna

Short answer: October to March. The longer answer depends on whether you are here to trek, swim, camp or hide from the world for a week.

Best all-round: Nov–Feb Peak: Dec–Jan Avoid: Jun–Sep

The seasons

Gokarna by season

Post-monsoon to winter (October–February)

The coast dries out, the sea settles and the shacks reopen. Days sit around 28–33°C with cooler evenings, and rain becomes rare. This is the window for the beach trek, camping and every water activity. December and January are the busiest weeks — book travel and stays early.

Summer (March–May)

Humidity climbs and midday heat is real, but the sea is often calm and prices soften. Walk and swim early, rest in the shade through the afternoon, and save day trips for the coast. By late May the pre-monsoon showers begin.

Monsoon (June–September)

The Western Ghats turn loud and green, waterfalls run full and Gokarna becomes a different place. But the sea is rough, most boats and water sports stop, many shacks close, and the trek's rocky sections are genuinely dangerous. Come for waterfalls and off-season calm, not beach time.

Festival dates to know

  • Maha Shivaratri (February/March) — the Mahabaleshwara chariot rolls down Car Street.
  • Ganesh Chaturthi (August/September) — processions and temple activity.
  • Diwali (October/November) — a quieter coastal celebration.
  • Christmas–New Year — peak season on the beaches; expect full shacks and higher rates.

Season by activity

  • 5-beach trek: October–March, starting by 8 AM.
  • Camping: October–March; camps close in the monsoon.
  • Boat rides and beach hopping: October–May, calmest December–February.
  • Watersports at Om: October–May.
  • Surfing at Main Beach: October–February for beginners.
  • Waterfalls and Yana: July–January, best right after the monsoon.
  • Temple festivals: Maha Shivaratri (Feb/Mar) and Ganesh Chaturthi (Aug/Sep).

What to pack, by season

Year-round: reef-safe sunscreen, insect repellent, a hat, grippy footwear and cash. Add a light rain shell and quick-dry layers in the monsoon, and a warm layer for December–January evenings. Full trek packing list: what to carry on the beach trek.

Quick picks

  • Best overall: November–February
  • Best value: October and March
  • Best for waterfalls: July–September
  • Busiest: Christmas–New Year
  • Avoid for trekking: June–September

FAQs

Best time questions

What is the best month to visit Gokarna?

November, December, January and February are the best months — calm seas, open shacks and comfortable trekking weather. December and January are the busiest, so book stays early.

Is Gokarna worth visiting in the monsoon?

The monsoon from June to September is lush and dramatic, but rough seas close many shacks and water activities, and the beach trek becomes slippery and leech-prone. It suits waterfall and off-season travellers rather than beach trips.

When is the beach trek season in Gokarna?

October to March. Start by 8 AM and avoid the April–May heat and the June–September monsoon, when rocky sections are dangerous.

What is the weather like in Gokarna in December?

December is one of the best months — warm days around 30°C, cooler evenings, calm seas and almost no rain. It is also the busiest holiday period, so book accommodation and transport well in advance.
