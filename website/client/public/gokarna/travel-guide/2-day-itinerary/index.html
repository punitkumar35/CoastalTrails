Weekend-sized plan

Gokarna 2-Day Itinerary

Enough time to see the temple town, swim at Kudle and Om, walk the 5-beach trek and catch two sunsets. Best for weekenders arriving from Bengaluru or Goa.

Best season: Oct–Mar Walking: 8–12 km Pace: relaxed

Before you start

  • Base yourself at Kudle or Om so both sunsets and the trek start are a short walk away.
  • Arrive the night before if you can — this plan assumes you wake up in Gokarna, not on a bus.
  • Carry water and cash every day; there are no ATMs on the southern beaches.
  • Check the tide the evening before your trek day.

Trip shape

  • Day 1: town, temples, Kudle and Om, sunset
  • Day 2: 5-beach trek, beach lunch, second sunset
  • Optional: stay a third night by the beach

Day one

Temple town and the first sunset

  1. 7:00 AM — Temple town walk 2–3 hours

    Start early while it is cool. Visit the Mahabaleshwara Temple, the Kotiteertha tank and the hilltop Maha Ganapati Temple, then walk Car Street for coffee and breakfast. Dress modestly and remove footwear where required. More detail: temple town guide.

  2. 10:30 AM — Main Beach stroll 45 min

    Walk down to Gokarna Main Beach where the town meets the water. It is the start of the coastal footpath and a good place to see the surf schools set up. Currents are strong here — this is a walk, not a swim.

  3. 11:30 AM — Walk or ride to Kudle 20–30 min on foot

    Follow the path over the headland to Kudle Beach. Check in, drop your bags and settle into a shack for lunch. Swimming is generally calm by day, but there are no lifeguards.

  4. 2:00 PM — Afternoon at Om Beach 3–4 hours

    Walk the 40–60 minute clifftop path to Om, or take an auto around by road. Swim, watch the watersports, or simply claim a table at a café on the main crescent. This is also the moment to sort your boat options for tomorrow if you would rather not walk back from Paradise.

  5. 5:30 PM — Sunset at Kudle until dark

    Return to Kudle for the coast's classic sunset — the beach faces west and the drum circles start early. Dinner at a shack, then an early night before the trek.

Day two

The 5-beach trek and a second sunset

  1. 7:30 AM — Start the trek from Kudle 6–8 km · 4–6 hours

    Leave by 7:30–8 AM with 2–3 litres of water. The route climbs the headland to Om, then bends around to Half Moon, Paradise and Belekan. Full route, distances and safety: 5-beach trek guide.

  2. 9:00 AM — Swim stop at Half Moon 30–45 min

    Cross the rocks carefully near low tide and take a swim in the calm crescent. Seasonal shacks may be open for a drink; signal here is patchy to none.

  3. 11:00 AM — Paradise Beach 30–60 min

    Continue over the boulder traverse to Paradise. If you camped here, pack up and carry everything out. From Paradise you can either finish the walk to Belekan or take a boat back toward Om.

  4. 1:00 PM — Finish at Belekan and return auto 20–30 min

    Walk up to the road at Belekan and take an auto back to Gokarna, Kudle or Om. Agree the fare first. Alternative: a boat from Paradise or Belekan to Om, roughly ₹150–₹300 per person. Lunch on arrival — you will have earned it.

  5. 4:00 PM — Slow afternoon by the water flexible

    Swim, nap or take a surf lesson at Main Beach. If you have an evening bus or train, shower and check out in good time — the station is about 10 km from town.

  6. 5:30 PM — Final sunset until departure

    Watch the sun drop one more time at Kudle or Om, then head out. If you can, stay a third night and add a day trip — see the 3-day itinerary.

What this trip costs

Excluding travel to Gokarna, budget roughly ₹2,000–₹3,000 per person per day for a stay, food and local transport, or less if you are hostel-minded and eating at local joints. Add ₹150–₹300 if you take a boat and ₹500–₹1,500 if you do watersports. Full breakdown: Gokarna trip cost.

Swap-ins if you have more time

  • Goa side-trip: Gokarna is only about 140 km from Panaji.
  • Mirjan Fort: 21 km north, easy to add to a departure day.
  • Camping: trade the second night for a tent at Half Moon or Paradise.

Packing reminders

  • Grippy footwear for the trek
  • 2–3 L water per person
  • Sun hat and reef-safe sunscreen
  • Cash in small notes
  • Power bank and dry bag

FAQs

Two-day trip questions

Is 2 days enough for Gokarna?

Two days are enough for the essentials — the temple town, Kudle and Om beaches and the 5-beach trek. You will not fit day trips to Mirjan Fort, Yana or Murudeshwar without adding a third day.

What should I not miss in Gokarna in two days?

The 5-beach trek from Kudle to Belekan is the experience to prioritise, along with sunset at Kudle or Om and a morning in the temple town.

How much does a 2-day Gokarna trip cost?

Excluding travel to Gokarna, budget about ₹2,000–₹3,000 per person per day for a stay, food and local transport, depending on the property and season.
