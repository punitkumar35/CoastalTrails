Plan by trip length

One day: temple town in the morning, Kudle and Om in the afternoon, sunset at Kudle. Two days: add the 5-beach trek. Three days: add camping or a Mirjan Fort and Yana day trip. Four days or more: slow down — surf lessons, mangrove kayaking, Murudeshwar and long shack afternoons.

Once your dates are set, find a stay close to the beach you want to wake up on. Coastal Trails lists family-run homestays at Kudle, Om, Half Moon, Paradise and Main Beach, with a 20% online hold and the balance paid at the property.

Gokarna quick facts

  • State: Karnataka, Uttara Kannada
  • Nearest rail: Gokarna Road (GOK), ~10 km
  • Best season: October–March
  • Ideal trip: 2–3 days
  • Trek: 6–8 km, 4–6 hours
  • Cash: carry it — few ATMs, patchy signal
