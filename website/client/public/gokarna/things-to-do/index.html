Things to Do in Gokarna

Gokarna is small, but it fills days easily: a cliff trek, a temple town, surf lessons, mangrove kayaking, secret sea caves and a set of genuinely worthwhile day trips.

Best season: October–March 2–3 days recommended No lifeguards

Culture

Temple town: Mahabaleshwara and around

Gokarna's identity starts at the Mahabaleshwara Temple, home of the Atmalinga and one of the oldest Shiva shrines on the west coast. The lanes around it — Car Street, the Kotiteertha tank and the hilltop Maha Ganapati Temple — make an easy, rewarding morning walk.

How to visit respectfully

  • Dress modestly: shoulders and knees covered, no beachwear. Some shrines have their own rules — follow the signage.
  • Remove footwear before entering temple premises and leave leather items outside.
  • Phones away inside shrines; ask before photographing people or rituals.
  • If a priest offers a blessing or thread, it is customary to offer a small donation — decide the amount yourself.
  • Walk clockwise around the sanctum, and follow the local queue system.

Time your visit for Maha Shivaratri (February/March), when the temple chariot rolls down Car Street — the town's biggest festival. Ganesh Chaturthi (August/September) is the other major date.

Good to know

  • Best time: 6–9 AM, before the heat and crowds
  • Distance: the temple town is a 15-minute walk from Main Beach
  • Footwear: expect to remove it often — wear slip-ons
  • Festivals: Maha Shivaratri, Ganesh Chaturthi
  • Food: Car Street has simple veg tiffin spots and coffee

By mood

More ways to fill a day

Sunrise and sunset spots

Sunset belongs to Kudle — the whole beach turns west and the drum circles start early. Om Beach is the other classic, especially from the southern rocks. For sunrise, walk to Main Beach or climb the headland between Kudle and Om for the first light on the water.

The sea caves below Kudle

At low tide, the headland between Kudle and Om opens small granite sea caves. Go at low tide, go with a local, and go before noon while the light still reaches inside. Sturdy footwear and a head torch help.

Surfing at Main Beach

Surf schools work the long, wave-friendly shoreline near town, with board rentals and lessons straight off the sand. The gentlest season runs October to February, and mornings usually have the cleanest waves.

Dolphin watching near Tadri

Indo-Pacific humpback dolphins feed where the Aghanashini meets the sea. Early-morning and sunset boat rides give the best sightings in season — a genuinely local operation rather than a staged show.

Yoga and slow mornings

Kudle's morning shalas and Om's sunset circles are the coast's quiet ritual. Drop-in classes run in season; bring your own mat if you are particular, or borrow one at your stay.

Konkani food on Car Street

Eat jolada rotti with a fiery chutney, payasa in the temple lanes, and pomfret rava-fry or solkadi at the beach shacks. The coast's cuisine is its own thing — coconut, kokum and rice, not generic Indian.
