The Coast, in Brief

A temple town that never stopped being a beach town

Gokarna sits where the Western Ghats meet the Arabian Sea, about 480 km from Bengaluru and 140 km south of Goa. Its name means “cow's ear”, and its Mahabaleshwara temple has drawn pilgrims for centuries. What makes it unusual is that the pilgrim shore and the traveller coast sit on the same footpath: Gokarna Main Beach leads to Kudle, then Om, Half Moon, Paradise and Belekan in a chain of coves you can walk end to end.

That walk — the Gokarna beach trek — is the reason many people come. The rest stay for the shack culture on Kudle and Om, tent nights at Half Moon and Paradise, temple mornings in town, and day trips to the laterite ramparts of Mirjan Fort or the limestone towers of Yana.

It is not a resort destination. Expect seasonal shacks, patchy mobile signal south of Om, no lifeguards on the wild beaches and a pace that rewards people who slow down.

Where Gokarna fits, by traveller

  • First-timers: 2 days for the temple town, Kudle and Om, plus the trek.
  • Trekkers: 3 days with the full 5-beach route and camping at Paradise.
  • Slow travellers: a week, mixing beach time, yoga, surfing and day trips.
  • Pilgrims: Mahabaleshwara, Maha Ganapati and Kotiteertha in a morning, ideally around Shivaratri.

Gokarna at a glance

  • State: Karnataka, Uttara Kannada district
  • Nearest railway: Gokarna Road (GOK), ~10 km
  • Nearest airports: Dabolim / Mopa (Goa), ~150–160 km
  • Best season: October–March
  • Ideal trip: 2–3 days
  • Beaches: Main, Kudle, Om, Half Moon, Paradise, Nirvana, Belekan
  • Known for: beach trek, temple town, camping

01Best time to visit

October to March is the sweet spot. November to February brings calm seas, open shacks and the best trekking weather; December and January are the busiest weeks, so book stays early. Month-by-month guide →

02How to reach Gokarna

Trains on the Konkan Railway stop at Gokarna Road, about 10 km from town. From Goa, buses take roughly 3–4 hours; from Bengaluru, overnight services run 10–12 hours. Full directions →

03Where to stay

Kudle and Om have the widest choice of shacks and guesthouses; the town is best for temple access. Coastal Trails lists family-run homestays across five beach enclaves with a 20% online hold.

04Suggested itineraries

Backpackers typically spend ₹1,800–₹2,500 a day; a comfortable trip runs ₹3,500–₹6,000 a day for two. Boats cost ₹150–₹300 and watersports ₹300–₹1,500. Full cost breakdown →

Gokarna travel questions

Is Gokarna worth visiting?

Yes. Gokarna pairs a living temple town with a walkable chain of beaches — Kudle, Om, Half Moon, Paradise and Belekan — plus day trips to Mirjan Fort, Yana and Murudeshwar. It suits travellers who want coast and culture without Goa's crowds.

How many days do you need in Gokarna?

Two days cover the temple town and the 5-beach trek. Three days add beach time, camping or a day trip to Mirjan Fort, Yana or Murudeshwar. Add a fourth day if you want to slow down.

Is the Gokarna beach trek difficult?

It is easy to moderate. Roughly 6–8 km with rocky clifftop sections and a few steep ups and downs, but no technical climbing. Most reasonably fit walkers finish in 4–6 hours with stops.

When is the best time to visit Gokarna?

October to March, with November to February offering calm seas, open shacks and comfortable trekking weather. The monsoon is lush but rough at sea, and April to May is hot and humid.

How do you get to Gokarna?

Gokarna Road railway station is about 10 km from town. The nearest airports are Dabolim and Mopa in Goa (roughly 150–160 km), and Hubballi (about 165 km). Overnight buses run from Bengaluru, Goa and Mangaluru.
